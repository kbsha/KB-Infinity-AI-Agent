The sun sets slow and paints the sky,\nA fiery hue that makes me sigh,\nThe stars come out and twinkle bright,\nA night of rest, a peaceful sight,\nThe world is hushed, a quiet place,\nWhere dreams are made and worries erase,\nThe moon is full, its gentle light,\nGuides me through the dark of night,\nI feel my heart, my soul, my mind,\nAt peace, in this world, I am free.
